Delete your Djinny account and data

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Djinny does not have its own account system or server. There is no Djinny username or password to delete. The only account involved is your own Google Account, used through Google Play Games Services if you choose to sign in.

If you signed in with Google Play Games

Signing in shares your Play Games player ID, display name, and profile icon with the app, used to show your name and avatar and to submit your scores to leaderboards and achievements. This is Google's data, held under your Google Account, not on any server of ours.

To delete it, use Google's own Play Games deletion tools:

  • On the web: go to play.google.com/games/profile, then Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account and Data. You can delete just Djinny's game data, or your entire Play Games profile.
  • On Android: open the Play Games app, tap More, then Settings, then Delete Play Games account and data, and confirm.

Full instructions are on Google's own support page: Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

Data stored on your device

Outside of your Google Account data described above, Djinny stores a small amount of data locally on your device only (your music preference and quiz settings). This is never sent anywhere, and it is removed immediately when you uninstall the app. There is no separate request or retention period needed for this, uninstalling deletes it right away.
